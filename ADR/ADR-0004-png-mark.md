# ADR-0004: The mark ships as a PNG

Status: Accepted (2026-10-28)

## Context

- The original plan (row BOOSTER-32 in the coordination repo's research notes) asked for the brand board and a
  single-color SVG mark readable at 16px, with avatar and favicon derived from it.
- The mark was generated by ChatGPT: a syringe that is also a rocket, in cream with one gold accent on the green
  tile. It arrived as raster images, and the front page needed avatars (1024, 512, 256), favicons (16, 32, 64 plus
  `.ico`) and the brand board.
- Nobody draws the mark and nobody edits its geometry: the PNGs are the mark, as `site/brand/README.md` says.

## Decision

1. The mark ships as the PNG set in `site/brand/`: `booster-avatar-1024.png`, `booster-avatar-512.png`,
   `booster-avatar-256.png`, `favicon-64.png`, `favicon-32.png`, `favicon-16.png`, `favicon.ico` and
   `booster-board.png`, the 11-panel board with lockups, palette, type and do and don't.
2. The site consumes the PNGs directly: favicon links, apple-touch-icon, Open Graph image, header and hero marks
   (`site/index.html`).
3. Do not redraw or edit the mark. If a vector is ever needed, trace the PNG with vtracer or potrace: the trace is
   a derivative, and the PNG stays canonical.

Why raster: every size the mark actually serves (a 16px favicon to a 512px avatar, a 36px header mark) is raster
territory, the generator produced raster, and making the PNGs canonical means every consumer agrees by
construction. Inferred: the 16px legibility goal from the original plan is met by the dedicated `favicon-16.png`
rather than by a scalable vector.

## Consequences

- There is no editable vector source in the repo. The geometry is frozen, and the board document is the reference
  for color and usage.
- New consumers take a file from `site/brand/` instead of asking for a new asset.
- A future vector is a trace, not a redesign.
